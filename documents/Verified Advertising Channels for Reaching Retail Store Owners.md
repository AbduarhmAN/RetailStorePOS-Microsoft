## Final Recommendation

The best channel mix for reaching **retail store business owners, small-business decision makers, and POS / retail-management-software buyers** is:

**Start with a high-intent paid core:**  
**Google Search Ads**, **LinkedIn Ads**, and **software comparison sites** such as **Capterra / Software Advice / G2**. This is the highest-confidence starting mix because it aligns with explicit buying intent, retail-software evaluation behavior, and B2B paid-channel effectiveness data. citeturn13search0turn22view0turn22view4

**Support that core with scaled paid social:**  
Use **Facebook and Instagram** for demand creation, retargeting, founder/operator reach, and creative testing. Meta’s scale among businesses and Intuit’s SMB advertising data make this the most practical broad-reach layer after search. citeturn39search1turn27view0turn8view0

**Build an organic trust layer in parallel:**  
Prioritize **Google Business Profile / local SEO**, a credible **LinkedIn company and founder presence**, product education on **YouTube**, and a strong footprint on **software review sites**. Retail-software purchases are high-friction enough that buyers will usually verify the vendor in multiple places before conversion. citeturn31search2turn22view0turn22view4turn8view0

**Use telecom and messaging selectively, not as the first cold-acquisition bet:**  
For **email**, the data justify serious investment immediately; for **SMS / WhatsApp / RCS**, I would reserve spend for opted-in lists, webinar audiences, demo follow-up, partners, installers, and reseller ecosystems.
